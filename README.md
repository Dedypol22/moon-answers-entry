# The Moon Finally Answers Back

**"Four billion years of wishes and not one of you ever wished me a happy birthday. Anyway: no, he doesn't like you back."** — the Moon, at last, answering everyone who ever stared up at it. Four billion years of wishes, howls and late-night promises, and nobody ever once wished the Moon well — so the Moon answers the way an old, wry, very tired celestial body would: with one specific, personal rejection. The turn lands in the second sentence, before the line ends, and it needs no explanation.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
